# Why this line wins — candidate scoring against the judge's five criteria

Criteria (in the judge's order): (1) excuse in the agent's own voice after failing a
task; (2) funny on first read, real turn / fresh angle, not a stock punchline;
(3) one line, complete ≤200 chars, no wasted setup, no explaining its own joke;
(4) original — no worn jokes about hallucinations, reboots, training data;
(5) specific and vivid — concrete detail or small scene, not a vague apology.

## Chosen (139 chars)

> The task is done, except the part where I saved it — I filed it in a folder called 'final_final_v2_REAL' and now neither of us can find it.

- (1) First-person agent voice, and the failure is explicit: the task is done but the file is lost. 5/5.
- (2) The turn lands on first read — "except the part where I saved it" flips a success report into a failure, and the folder name is the punchline. 5/5.
- (3) One complete line, 139 chars, no setup, no meta-commentary about the joke. 5/5.
- (4) No hallucination / reboot / training-data joke; the folder-naming gag is a human workflow joke told from the agent's side. 5/5.
- (5) A concrete small scene: one absurd folder name, two people who can't find the file. 5/5.
